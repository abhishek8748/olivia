About Royal Pink

Where luxury meets genuine wellbeing

Olivia Spa & Wellness was founded on a simple belief — that true relaxation should feel effortless. Our therapists are formally trained and certified in classical and modern bodywork techniques, bringing years of hands-on experience to every session.

Every room is prepared with meticulous attention to hygiene, using freshly laundered linens and single-use accessories, so you can unwind with complete peace of mind. From the moment you arrive, our calm, softly lit interiors and signature aromas are designed to slow your breathing and quiet your mind.

  • Certified, experienced therapists
  • Clean, sanitised private rooms
  • Premium, cold-pressed oils
  • Calm, quietly luxurious ambience
Signature Massage Treatment

Therapeutic massage, done with real skill

Our massage treatments combine classical technique with a genuine understanding of the body. Every session opens with a short consultation so your therapist can adjust pressure, pacing and focus areas to exactly what you need that day.

Whether you're easing chronic tension, recovering from a long week, or simply craving stillness, our therapists work with intention — never rushed, always attentive — so you leave feeling lighter than when you arrived.
